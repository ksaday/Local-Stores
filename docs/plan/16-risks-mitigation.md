R19, R20, R21, and R24 share one structural mitigation: **the human approval gates, now split across two people** — the SuperAdmin approving during the run, and the owner accepting at sign-off. An "auto-approve everything" mode, or a sign-off collapsed to a single button, removes the primary defense for all four at once and requires an ADR that says so explicitly.

The SuperAdmin-only operator model is a deliberate trade: it removes the coordination cost that would stall onboarding (R5) at the price of concentrating owner comprehension into one step (R23) and putting the platform temporarily in an operator role (R25). Both costs are paid down by the same control — a sign-off that is a real walkthrough.

## 16.2 The four risks that decide the project

**R1 (isolation) and R2 (payments) are existential** — a single incident of either can end the business, because the entire value proposition is "your store, your money, your customers, kept separate." Both are mitigated with *tests that block releases*, not with review checklists. If the CI isolation suite is ever disabled to unblock a deploy, that is itself the incident.

**R3 (checkout complexity) is the most likely schedule risk.** Checkout is where pricing, coupons, tax, stock reservation, order numbering, idempotency, and the state machine all meet. The plan de-risks it structurally: it is the only XL phase, it demos with cash payments so it doesn't wait on Stripe, and its transaction boundaries are pre-specified rather than discovered mid-sprint.

**R5 (adoption) is the most likely business risk** and the one engineering can least fix late. It is why onboarding gets a wizard, a CSV import, and a publish checklist in Phase 4–5 rather than "after launch," and why the pilot cohort is deliberately small enough to support by hand.

## 16.3 Review cadence

The register is reviewed at every phase boundary (§15): re-score, retire what's passed, add what the phase revealed. Any risk scoring ≥ 15 gets a named owner and a written mitigation plan before the next phase starts.
